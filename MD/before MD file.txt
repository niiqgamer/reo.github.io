build a web browser game, mmorpg & idle, 2.5D quarter view similar to Ragnarok Online and whole arts is 2.5D anime-style chibi-proportional semi-realism, inspired by Tree of Savior, can swap horizontal (if on desktop web app) and vartical (if on mobile web or app) mode, main language is THAI and ENGLISH (can swap anytime with no need to restart), all new players start wihtin 'Gadolinium' - a town of begining, and test with character at the center of the map, camera always follow the character at center, can click anywhere on map and walk to, mockup UI

Core Loop:
1. Kill monsters
2. Get Base & Job EXPs, Items, Coins
3. Level Up
4. Better Equipments = Better CP (Refine Equipments & Card Slot & Equipments Options)
5. Do Quests
6. Instance Boss
7. Daily Dungeons
8. Idle Farming for Materials

Meta Loop:
Future updates.

How to change jobs:
1. At new game, everyone is a Beginner (called 'C0')
2. Leveling to Base Level at 10+ & Job Level at Max(10)
3. Do an archetype change quest, change to 1st Class (called 'C1')
4. Leveling to Base Level at 50+ & Job Level Max(50)
5. Do an upper classes change quest, change to 2nd Class (called 'C2')
6. Leveling to Base Level Max(99)** & Job Level Max(50)
**The current level cap is set at 99 and will be expanded in future updates.

Major Currencies:
1. Scandium: Common In-game Gold (General trading & NPC fees)
2. Yttrium: Untradable In-game Cash (Earnable bound currency)
3. Lanthanum: Tradable Premium Cash (Player trading & Premium shop)
